# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Be respectful, constructive, and technical.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith; disagree plainly and without contempt.
- No harassment, trolling, personal attacks, or publishing private
  information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior via a private channel (GitHub advisory or the
contact listed on the org profile). Maintainers will review and take
proportionate action: warning, temporary restriction, or removal from the
community.

Project maintainers who fail to uphold these standards may be removed.
